Who Am I?

My name is Huw. My pronouns are they/them. I'm originally from London but have lived in Leeds for the last decade or so.

Organisations & Qualifications

I am a member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy. This is the professional body that ensures its members are operating ethically to a set of standards and values.

I am part of the Free Psychotherapy Network, and as such I offer a number of free slots for counselling. If the cost of therapy is a barrier to you, please let me know.

I typically work Mondays to Thursdays, online on Zoom and in person in Leeds.
To get in touch for a free initial chat, send me an email any time, or give me a call between the hours of 9am and 5pm.
