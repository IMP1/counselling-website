How Do I Work?

My practice is based on a person-centred foundation that places you as the expert of your own life and experiences. Upon that, I often work with systemic and systematic issues, from family dynamics to societal issues.

I bring my lived experiences, my knowledge, and my therapy experience, and you bring your lived experiences, your thoughts, feelings, and behaviours, and everything that makes you you. And together we work towards your goals for our therapy.

What Can You Expect?

Initial Chat

The most important aspect for successful therapy is finding a therapist that works for you. For some people, having a therapist share a personal understanding of their situation is helpful, while for others it might hinder the work.

I am very happy to see if we'll be a good fit, and work well together no matter who you are and where you're coming from, and what you're coming with. I also have particular experience and training working with people bringing issues around the following:

  • Anxiety
  • Depression
  • LGBTQ+ Counselling
  • Loneliness
  • Neurodiversity
  • Self-Harm
  • Suicide

I typically work Mondays to Thursdays, online on Zoom and in person in Leeds.
To get in touch for a free initial chat, send me an email any time, or give me a call between the hours of 9am and 5pm.
